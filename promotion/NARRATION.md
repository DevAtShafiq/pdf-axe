## Plain text (for a human voice-over)

Meet Office Axe — a Windows desktop workspace for preparing student documents.

Type or paste a list of names, and Office Axe creates a folder for every student. The live preview flags duplicates and invalid names before anything is made.

Every file previews right inside the app — Word documents and PDFs alike.

To rename, just start typing, and pick a ready-made English and Korean document name.

Select Word files and convert them to PDF. The work runs in the background with a progress card, and the originals are always kept.

Arrange pages visually. Delete a blank page — changed your mind? Control Z brings it back.

Fix a sideways scan, drag the cover letter to the front, and save it as a new file.

Office Axe. Student documents, sorted.
