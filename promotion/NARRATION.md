## Plain text (for a human voice-over)

This is Office Axe — one desktop app for everything an education office does with student documents. Let's take a tour.

Office Axe opens like a file explorer. Browse your folders, switch between a list and thumbnails, sort, and search as you type.

Select any file to see its details, and pick a light or a dark theme.

Need a folder for every student? Paste the list of names, add numbering if you like, and the live preview shows exactly what will be created.

Files preview instantly inside the app — Word documents, Excel sheets, photos and PDFs, with zoom — no other program needed.

Rename with ready-made English and Korean document names. Press F2, start typing, and pick a template.

You can edit the template list yourself.

Cut and paste files between student folders. Every step can be undone with Control Z, and redone with Control Y.

Convert Word documents to PDF in the background, and the originals are always kept.

Combine phone photos of pages into one PDF, or turn PDF pages into images.

Compress big scans to a target size, like one megabyte. Office Axe estimates the result for each setting before you start.

Merge documents into one PDF.

Split a long file every few pages, or click the pages you want to extract.

Arrange pages visually. Delete a blank page — Control Z brings it back — rotate a sideways scan, and drag pages into order.

The PDF editor fills in real form fields.

Draw your signature and drop it on the line. Fit a passport photo into the photo box, then save a signed copy.

Mark up scans and photos with highlights, boxes and arrows.

Make passport and visa photos at the exact size — here, thirty-five by forty-five millimetres — with a face guide, a file-size limit and a four-by-six print sheet.

Need a formal photo? With your own OpenAI key, the AI tool dresses a casual portrait in a suit and tie. Then compare before and after.

Click QR, then click any QR code on your screen. Links open in your browser, and text is shown right away.

Zip files with a password, or lock a PDF so it needs a password to open.

There's a built-in screen recorder, and you can grab a screenshot of any region.

Your plan, devices and payment history live on the Subscription page, and Settings keeps your folders, theme and AI key.

Office Axe. Student documents, sorted.
